How to Play

🏠
The Basics
Every round, a plane takes off and a multiplier climbs from 1.00x upward. Place a bet before the round starts, then cash out any time while the plane is flying to lock in your stake × the multiplier at that moment. If the plane "flies away" (crashes) before you cash out, that bet is lost.
Placing a Bet
  • Enter an amount, or tap one of the quick amount buttons.
  • Tap INVEST before the countdown ends - bets can't be placed once the round is already flying.
  • You can run two bets at once, one in each betting slot.
Cashing Out
Once the round is flying, your bet button turns into a CASH OUT button showing your current payout. Tap it any time before the crash to collect that amount. There's no "right" moment - cashing out earlier is safer, waiting longer can pay more, but risks the round crashing first.
Auto Bet & Auto Cash Out
Switch a betting slot to the Auto tab to automate it:
  • Auto Bet - automatically places the same bet amount at the start of every new round.
  • Auto Cash Out - automatically cashes out once the multiplier reaches the value you set.
Both can be turned off at any time from the same panel.
Provably Fair
Every round's outcome is generated in a way you can verify independently. Tap Verify on the fairness bar above the graph to see the current round's seed and hash, or look up any past round's result.
Play Responsibly
Only bet amounts you're comfortable losing, and take breaks between sessions. If you ever feel like play is becoming a problem, that's worth stepping back for.
